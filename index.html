Frequently asked questions

How does Scan & Pay risk checking work?

Choose a payment method, scan a supported QR or enter recipient details, and add the amount. FinSight shows a demo risk level with the reasons it flagged. Confirm the recipient and amount in your bank app before making any real payment.

Can FinSight send money or access my bank?

No. This prototype does not connect to a bank or payment gateway. Proceed to payment only simulates the next step; no money is transferred. Never enter your UPI PIN or OTP here.

How do I reconcile an invoice?

Open Reconciliation and choose a file. The hackathon demo shows sample extracted invoice details and stages a sample payment for risk review. Actual OCR, accounting imports and payment initiation require secure backend integrations.

Why did a transaction get a medium or high risk score?

The assessment considers demo signals such as recipient verification, selected payment mode, transaction amount, unusual patterns and unverified feedback. It is a review aid, not proof that a payment is safe or fraudulent.

Where can I see all transaction details?

Open Transaction History at the bottom of the navigation and select “View details” beside a transaction. The detail view includes recipient, payment method, reference, amount, risk reasons and reconciliation status.

How do I update my profile or preferences?

Use My Profile to update your name and contact details. Settings contains notification and safety preferences. Changes in this prototype are local demo interactions and are not synced to a server.

How do I leave feedback about FinSight?

Open Website Reviews to read or submit feedback about the FinSight website experience. Reviews in this prototype are stored in the page only.
